## 2. Summary

- **Your videos stay on your device.** We do not upload your recordings, clips or reels.

- **Location is optional.** It is never tracked in the background.
- We do **not** sell your data, show ads or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## 3. Data we process

### 3.1 Account
When you sign up, we collect:
- **Username** (required)
- **Email address** (required)
- **Phone number** (optional)
- **App-generated device ID**: a random identifier that is created by the app and stored in your device's Keychain. It is *not* the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA).
- **Registration time**

The app regularly checks with our server whether your account is still active.

### 3.2 Support requests
If you contact us through **Settings > Support**, we receive:
- the request type (issue, idea or other)
- your subject and message
- your name and email, if you enter them
- your user ID

### 3.3 Analytics (only with your consent)
If you choose **Share analytics**, the app sends usage events. Examples are app open and close, starting a match, saving a clip, changing a setting, and error messages.

Each event includes:
- your user ID
- the time of the event
- app version and build
- iOS version
- device model (for example "iPhone")

Nothing is sent if you choose **Don't share** or turn analytics off later. Events that are waiting to be sent are deleted when you turn analytics off.

### 3.4 Location (optional)
If you allow location access ("While Using the App"), PadelCam uses your location on your device to pin matches and clips on a map. Apple's geocoding service turns the coordinates into an address.

If you have **also** opted in to analytics, your approximate current coordinates (latitude/longitude) are attached to the analytics events. PadelCam never starts location tracking just for analytics and never tracks location in the background.

### 3.5 App integrity
To protect our server from abuse, the app uses Apple's **App Attest**. App Attest sends us a cryptographic key identifier and proofs that requests come from a genuine copy of PadelCam. It contains no personal content.

### 3.6 Data that stays on your device
These are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.
- **Camera and microphone:** recordings, clips and reels. You decide whether to save them to Photos or share them using the iOS share sheet.
- **Voice command:** this uses Apple's Speech Recognition framework to listen for your save keyword during a match. Apple may process the audio under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not receive the audio or the transcripts.
- **Hand gesture detection:** this happens on the device.
- **Apple Watch:** the Watch app only sends a "save" signal to your iPhone.
- **Settings and preferences.**

## 4. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR Art. 6)

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Creating and managing your account | Account data, device ID | Art. 6(1)(b), performance of contract |
| Answering support requests | Support data | Art. 6(1)(b), and Art. 6(1)(f), our legitimate interest in helping users |
| Improving the app and fixing errors | Analytics data, location attached to events | Art. 6(1)(a), consent. Can be withdrawn anytime |
| Protecting the service from abuse | App Attest data, device ID | Art. 6(1)(f), legitimate interest in security |

Device-level storage and access (for example the device ID, and analytics events waiting to be sent) is based on § 25 TDDDG. It is either strictly necessary or based on your consent.
